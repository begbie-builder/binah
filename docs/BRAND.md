# Binah: Bet and spark

The compact mark is a custom **ב (Bet)**, the first letter of בינה, with a six-point spark in the position of Bet’s interior dot. The broad roof, rounded shoulder, and projecting baseline echo the existing Hebrew wordmark. Its large opening and two simple shapes keep it legible when the full name cannot fit.

- `public/brand-mark.svg`: forest-green symbol on a transparent background. Use beside the full name, in narrow navigation, on progress rewards, or as the compact identity.
- `public/app-icon.svg`: cream symbol on a full-bleed forest-green square. Intended for exported favicon, PWA, and Apple touch icon assets; the operating system supplies its own corner mask.
- `public/logo-hebrew.svg`: the existing full Hebrew wordmark, preserved. Use for headers, landing pages, and places where the complete name fits.

The symbol is a companion identity, not a new spelling or a replacement for the wordmark. The internal star is a brand detail rather than a pronunciation mark. The icon needs neither a border nor a decorative container inside the app-icon square.

## Size and color

The 192-unit mark has a 26-unit primary stroke and generous clear space. At 24 pixels its strokes are about 3.25 pixels wide and its spark is about 4.25 pixels high. The Bet remains the main silhouette; at favicon sizes the spark simplifies to a small bright/dark point. Prefer 24 pixels or more for standalone navigation and 32 pixels or more when the star detail matters.

Forest green: `#254d43`. Cream: `#f6f4ed`. The monochrome artwork also works with a single accessible brand color on a contrasting plain background. Preserve its aspect ratio and clear space. Do not stretch it, add a gradient, add a shadow, or bake rounded app-icon corners into the source.

The SVG geometry is original project artwork. It does not depend on a font, raster image, external service, or proprietary logo asset.
